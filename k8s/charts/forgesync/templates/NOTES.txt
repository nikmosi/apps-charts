1. Get the list of created CronJobs by running:
  kubectl get cronjob -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}

2. To trigger an immediate manual job run for a target (e.g. github):
  kubectl create job --from=cronjob/{{ include "forgesync.fullname" . }}-github {{ include "forgesync.fullname" . }}-manual-run-$(date +%s)

3. View job logs:
  kubectl logs -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }} -f --tail=100
